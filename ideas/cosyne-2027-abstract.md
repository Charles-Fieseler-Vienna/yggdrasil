## Summary

"What would a generative model of worm behavior need from the brain?", submitted to Cosyne 2027 ([[@Cosyne 2027 abstract]]). Figure 1
asks what neurons add to posture forecasts; Figure 2 asks when they help.

## Findings

- Posture predicts neural activity only nonlinearly ([[CLM - Posture predicts neural activity only nonlinearly]]).
- Posture mostly predicts itself over seconds ([[CLM - Posture mostly predicts itself over seconds]]).
- Nonlinear posture models need data pooled across animals ([[CLM - Nonlinear posture models need data pooled across animals]]).
- Neurons add little to posture forecasts within a state ([[CLM - Neurons add little to posture forecasts within a state]]).
- Neurons help just after a state transition, but report the change late ([[CLM - Neurons improve posture forecasts just after a state transition]], [[CLM - Neurons report state changes late]]).
