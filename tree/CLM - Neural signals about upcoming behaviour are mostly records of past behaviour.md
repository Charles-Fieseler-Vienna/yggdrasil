## Claim

Neural signals that appear to predict upcoming behaviour are mostly lagged records of behaviour that already
happened.

## Rationale

Regime, next-bout and within-run signals vanish or shrink by ~80 % once neurons are residualized on behaviour history
([[EVD - Neural signals about upcoming behaviour reduce to past behaviour]]).

## Scope

36-animal cohort, linear residualization.
