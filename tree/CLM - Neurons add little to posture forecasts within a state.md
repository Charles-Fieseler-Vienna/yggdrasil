## Claim

Within a behavioural state, whole-brain activity adds little to forecasts of future posture beyond posture itself.

## Rationale

Gains over a shifted null are ≤ 0.01 at the forecast time and ≤ 0.05 as an ongoing input, with a test that detects
realistic simulated signals ([[EVD - Neurons add at most 0.05 R2 to posture forecasts]], Fig 1g, h of [[@Cosyne 2027 abstract]]).

## Scope

36-animal cohort, nuclear GCaMP at 3.5 Hz. Around state transitions the picture differs.
