## Claim

Most of the next second or two of posture is predictable from posture alone.

## Rationale

Linear posture history reaches R² 0.61 at 1.4 s, and two frames capture most of it ([[EVD - Posture history forecasts posture at 1.4 s with R2 about 0.6]], Fig 1e of
[[@Cosyne 2027 abstract]]).

## Scope

Freely moving worms, 28 test animals. Predictability drops to R² ~0.2 at 4.3 s and is worst around state changes.
