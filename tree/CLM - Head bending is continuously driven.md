## Claim

Head bending is driven continuously at the head, and the drive then propagates down the body as the wave.

## Rationale

Head innovations are not concentrated in events (48 % against 47 % of energy in the top 10 % of frames for an
event-free surrogate), travel at the wave speed and decorrelate in ~130 ms ([[EVD - Head innovations are continuous and travel down the body]]).

## Scope

Forward locomotion in the 36-animal cohort and its 12 ms re-export. Whether the drive is neural is not settled at
3.5 Hz imaging.
