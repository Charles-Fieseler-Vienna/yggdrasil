## Claim

Whole-brain activity reflects a new behavioural state about a second after the switch, not before.

## Rationale

State information in neurons arrives 0.6–1.2 s after the switch and no faster readout helps ([[EVD - Neural information about a new state arrives 0.6 to 1.2 s late]]).

## Scope

Nuclear GCaMP at 3.5 Hz; indicator and neural delay are not separated.
