## Question

Can linear posture dynamics within forward runs be split by differential observability into a locomotion subspace
(observed by speed y1), a navigation subspace (observed by steering y2) and a silent subspace, such that the navigation
subspace is stable across animals, loads on the anterior body, and is neurally encoded beyond the FWD/REV manifold?

## Context

The original core claim of the Cosyne_2027 project (`Cosyne_2027@0164258:PLAN.md`). The project left this track on
2026-10-13, when its bars were missed and its neural targets did not decode, and moved to neural modulation of posture
models. The control-decomposition feasibility sprint revisits it (`Cosyne_2027@0164258:research/NEXT_STEPS.md`, S1).
