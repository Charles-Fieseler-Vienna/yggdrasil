## Claim

Recent posture predicts a modest share of whole-brain activity, but only through a nonlinear map.

## Rationale

An MLP from 6 frames of posture explains 15 % of neural variance; a linear map explains ≈ 0 ([[EVD - An MLP from posture history explains 15 percent of neural variance]], Fig 1b, c of
[[@Cosyne 2027 abstract]]).

## Scope

28 test animals; the explained variance is mostly the global PC1 state. Behaviour features that include speed and
state predict neural activity linearly.
