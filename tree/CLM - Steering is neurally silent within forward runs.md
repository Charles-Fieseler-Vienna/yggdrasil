## Claim

Within forward runs, steering, speed and the navigation subspace cannot be decoded from whole-brain activity.

## Rationale

Linear decoders recover none of them on 36 animals, while anterior curvature decodes well in the same data
([[EVD - Neural decoding of observability targets within forward runs]]).

## Scope

Forward runs only; linear decoders; nuclear GCaMP at 3.5 Hz. Steering across states (turns, reversals) is not
covered.
