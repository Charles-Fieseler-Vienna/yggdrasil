## Claim

Linear alignment of neurons across a handful of animals recovers the shared FWD/REV state but adds nothing to an
animal's own neural model.

## Rationale

Zero-shot transfer reaches R² 0.30 against 0.43 within animal and is flat in the number of training animals
([[EVD - Neural alignment across animals gives no gain at 5 to 10 animals]]).

## Scope

5–10 animals, linear alignment (OT + generalized Procrustes, PPCA imputation).
