## Claim

Neural activity improves posture forecasts in the first second after a behavioural state transition, where
posture-only models fail.

## Rationale

R² 0–1 s after onset rises from 0.00–0.03 to 0.22 with causal neurons; per animal the gain is +0.17
([[EVD - Neural gains concentrate in the second after a state onset]], Fig 2 of [[@Cosyne 2027 abstract]]).

## Scope

Pooled models on 28 test animals; within states the gain is ≤ 0.
