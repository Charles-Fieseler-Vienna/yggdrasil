## Claim

Nonlinear posture models beat linear history only when trained on data pooled across many animals.

## Rationale

Single-animal nonlinear models lose at every horizon; the pooled 12-D modulated model wins by +0.07–0.08 and passes
linear history between 4 and 18 training animals ([[EVD - Pooled modulated models pass linear history at 4 to 18 animals]], Fig 1e, i, j of [[@Cosyne 2027 abstract]]).

## Scope

36-animal cohort; deterministic forecasting models, not generative ones.
